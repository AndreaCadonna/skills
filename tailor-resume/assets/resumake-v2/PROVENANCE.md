# Resumake v2 provenance

These files are vendored from [saadq/resumake.io](https://github.com/saadq/resumake.io/tree/v2) at commit `0274664df69a9d55fa7548d497e6ef765b9f0435` on the `v2` branch.

Included material:

- `generators/template1.ts` through `template9.ts`: the original Resumake v2 TypeScript LaTeX generators.
- `templates/`: the original class, style, configuration, and font assets required by templates 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8.
- `common-tags/common-tags.min.cjs`: pinned `common-tags` 1.8.2, Resumake v2's string-template dependency.

Resumake's MIT notice is preserved in `LICENSE`. The license for `common-tags` is preserved in `common-tags/LICENSE.md`. Several templates, classes, styles, and fonts have separate terms. Their headers remain intact, and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) maps every bundled family to the applicable notice and included license text.

The Resumake README credits the Rensselaer Career Development Center, Byungjin Park, Scott Clark, Debarghya Das, Xavier Danaux, Ratul Saha, Daniil Belyakov, and Frits Wenneker as template creators or sources.
